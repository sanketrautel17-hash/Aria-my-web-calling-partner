# RBI Bank Loan Policy 2026

## Home Loan Interest Rates
- Standard Home Loan: 8.5% p.a.
- Women Borrowers: 8.25% p.a.
- Senior Citizens: 8.15% p.a.
- NRI Home Loan: 8.75% p.a.

## Loan Eligibility
- Age: 21 to 65 years
- Employment: Minimum 2 years in current organization
- Minimum Salary: ₹30,000 per month
- Credit Score: 750 or above for best rates

## Personal Loan Rates
- Salaried Employees: 10.5% - 15% p.a.
- Self-employed Professionals: 11% - 18% p.a.

## Terms and Conditions
- Processing Fee: 0.5% of loan amount
- Prepayment Charges: NIL for floating rate loans
- Repayment Tenure: Up to 30 years for Home Loans, 5 years for Personal Loans.
